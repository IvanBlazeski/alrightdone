What the app does

  • A list for every store

    Supermarket, bakery, pharmacy: each gets its own list, with quick suggestions that fit the store.

  • Tick it off, then “Alright, done!”

    Check items as they go in the basket. One tap when you’re finished, and the list is ready for next time.

  • A weekly goal you can keep

    Pick how often you shop and grow a streak. One free pause a month, because life happens.

  • Your usuals in one tapPRO

    Star the things you buy every week and add them all to the right list at once.

  • See what you buy most

    History keeps every finished shop, with how much you bought this week. Monthly stats come with Pro.

  • In your language

    English, German, French, Spanish and Italian, with metric or US units.

How it works

  1. 1

    Make a list

    One for each store, with suggestions that fit it. Add your own in a few taps.

  2. 2

    Tick things off

    Tap each item as it goes in the basket. You’ll see at a glance what’s still missing.

  3. 3

    Tap “Alright, done!”

    Your shop counts toward your weekly goal, and the list is ready for next time.

Hi, I’m Ivan! 👋

I built Alright, Done because I wanted a simple way to never forget anything when I go shopping, and to challenge myself to become a better designer and developer along the way.

I wanted a shopping list that feels genuinely enjoyable to use. Something as simple as tapping an item to mark it as bought should feel effortless and satisfying.

That’s why I spent so much time on the little details, from the clean, intuitive design to the small interactions that make everyday shopping a little easier and more fun.

I hope you feel that care when you use Alright, Done, and that it makes your shopping trips easier, just like it has for me.
