Privacy Policy

Last updated: 3 November 2026

Alright, Done doesn’t collect your data.

Your lists and everything else you enter stay on your iPhone. There’s no account, no ads and no tracking.

Who makes the app

Alright, Done is made by Ivan Blazheski, an independent developer in North Macedonia (“I”, “me”). For any privacy question, write to alrightdone.support@gmail.com.

What the app keeps, and where

The app saves what you enter so it can work: your lists, items, amounts and notes, your usual items, your finished shops and shopping stats, your streak and weekly goal, your settings, and your answers from the first-time setup, including your name if you choose to give one.

All of this is stored only on your iPhone, inside the app. It is never sent to me or to any server, and there is no account to sign in to.

Your name

You can type your name, write it with your finger, or skip it. If you write it, Apple’s text recognition reads the drawing on your iPhone. The drawing itself is never saved or sent anywhere; only the name it reads is kept, on your iPhone.

Reminders

Reminders are notifications your iPhone schedules for itself. They don’t go through any server.

Purchases

Alright, Done Pro is sold through Apple’s App Store. Apple handles the payment; I never see your card or payment details. The app asks Apple on your iPhone whether you have Pro. Apple’s privacy policy applies to purchases.

Analytics and crash reports

The app contains no analytics, advertising or tracking code. If you’ve chosen in your iPhone’s settings to share analytics with app developers, Apple may share crash reports and usage statistics with me. These don’t identify you, and I use them only to fix problems.

Backups

Depending on your iPhone’s settings, the app’s data can be part of your iCloud or computer backups. Those are handled by Apple and by you, not by me.

When you write to me

If you email me, I receive your email address and what you write. When you use Contact support or Send feedback in the app, the email also includes the app version, the iOS version and the app’s language, so I can help you; it never includes your lists or your name unless you type them. I use your email only to reply. It’s kept in my email account (Gmail, provided by Google) for as long as needed to help you, and I’ll delete it if you ask.

Deleting your data

Delete the app from your iPhone, and all of its data is deleted with it. Copies in your backups follow your backup settings. To have your emails to me deleted, just ask.

Your rights

Because the app’s data never reaches me, there’s nothing about you in the app that I could show, change or delete. For emails you’ve sent me, you can ask to see, correct or delete them at any time. If you live in the EU or the UK, you can also contact your data protection authority.

Children

The app doesn’t collect personal data from anyone, including children.

This website

This website uses no cookies, no analytics and no third-party fonts. It’s hosted by GitHub Pages, which may log technical data such as IP addresses to keep the service secure; see GitHub’s privacy statement.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, I’ll update this page and the date at the top. If the app ever starts handling your data differently, I’ll say so here before that version is released.
